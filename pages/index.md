# Cameras App

A native Android client for watching IP cameras on your own private network, over a WireGuard
tunnel you provide.

- **[Privacy policy](./privacy-policy/)** — what the app stores on your device, what it never
  collects, and why each permission is needed. In English and Portuguese.
- **[Infrastructure guide](./infra-guide/)** — the MikroTik router, the firewalled zones, the
  IPv6-only WireGuard VPN, the VPN peers and the Raspberry Pi's firewall, step by step from a
  blank router.
- **[Raspberry Pi services guide](./pi-services-guide/)** — the recordings disk, MediaMTX (live
  video for the app), the 24/7 recorder and Samba, continuing the infrastructure guide.
